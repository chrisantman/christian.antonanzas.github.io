A set of courses of the **Degree in Music** at the **Universidad Internacional de La Rioja (UNIR)**, where I am the responsible lecturer:

- **Sonorización** (Sound Reinforcement) — sound system design and electroacoustics.
- **Acústica de recintos** (Room Acoustics) — electroacoustic conditioning of home studios.
- **Grabación, mezcla y masterización** (Recording, Mixing and Mastering) — reorganized into ten units following the real flow of music production, from fundamentals and room design to spectral, dynamic and temporal processing, surround sound and mastering.
- **Edición de audio y MIDI** (Audio and MIDI Editing).
- **Síntesis de sonido** (Sound synthesis).

All courses are 6 ECTS, taught in online modality with large groups. I have incorporated professional AI activities (critical analysis of generative AI tools in acoustics and music production contexts) as one of the faculty's reference lecturers in professional AI.
