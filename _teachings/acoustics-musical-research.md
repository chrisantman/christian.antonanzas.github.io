Course of the **Master's in Musical Research** at the **Universidad Internacional de La Rioja (UNIR)**, structured into conceptual blocks: fundamentals of acoustics, psychoacoustics, musical and room acoustics, digital audio and analysis with Sonic Visualiser.

Designed to be accessible to music students and aligned with learning outcomes linked to understanding and applying sound in musical contexts.

- 6 ECTS, online modality.
- Materials and resources published under ISBN 978-84-19837-54-7.
- I also supervise the Master's final theses (TFM) of this programme.

Students' institutional evaluations for this and related courses average between 7.42 and 9.32 out of 10.
