Research line developed during my PhD at the Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV) and extended in collaboration with the KU Leuven and the GTAC group (iTEAM-UPV).

We design and implement **distributed and collaborative adaptive algorithms** (DMEFxLMS, DMEFeLMS, affine-projection and frequency-domain block variants) to control the sound field over networks of acoustic nodes (loudspeakers + microphones), creating personalized quiet/equalization zones for multiple users in the same enclosure.

Key competitive projects:

- **D-Noise** — *Distributed Network of Active Noise Equalizers for Multi-User Sound Control* (European Commission, H2020-FETOPEN, 2017–2018). I was responsible for the real-time implementation of ANC/ANE prototypes over collaborative acoustic nodes, culminating in a physical multi-user demonstration.
- **SSPressing** — *Smart Sound Processing for the Digital Living* (Ministry of Economy, TEC2015-67387-C4-1-R, 2016–2018). Distributed ANC algorithms in the time–frequency domain with diffusion and incremental cooperation strategies.
- **DisCoSound** — *Distributed and collaborative processing of sound signals* (TEC2012-38142-C04-01, 2014–2016). Adaptation of centralized LMS/MEFxLMS and affine-projection algorithms to distributed networks.

This work led to several JCR journal papers and international conference publications, and all the code and measurements are released as open source on GitHub.
