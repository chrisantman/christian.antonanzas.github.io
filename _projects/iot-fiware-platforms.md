As **Project Manager and Technical Project Manager at [Bosonit](https://www.bosonit.com/)** (2018–2021) I led R&D+I projects applying the European **FIWARE** platform to real-time IoT and data ecosystems.

Selected projects:

- **CULTIVO 4.0** (ADER) — predictive models of mushroom crop growth and production from historical data plus automated digital image processing.
- **FIFACE / FIRACE / FISTREAM** (ADER) — image recognition algorithms (faces, PPE), GPRS data acquisition, and video connectors integrated into the FIWARE ecosystem.
- **RIOGISTICS** (L4MS, H2020) — design, simulation and integration of AGV/CMR robots with OPIL + FIWARE.
- **AFARCLOUD** (ECSEL) — data analysis and visualization.
- **FIWINE** (Bodegas Franco-Españolas) — presence sensors, production measurement and task-planning algorithms.
- **CONSORCIO PEQUEÑO** (SCPSA), **CLUSTER DE AUTOMOCIÓN** (Hiberus), **CLOUDIFICACIÓN** (AERTIC/Arsys) — FIWARE data integration, IoT agents, NGSIv2 data modelling and real-time dashboards.

As a certified **FIWARE Expert**, I also designed and taught a 20-hour introductory FIWARE course at the Centro Nacional de Formación en Nuevas Tecnologías de La Rioja, whose materials are openly available in [GitHub](https://github.com/chrisantman/curso-fiware).
