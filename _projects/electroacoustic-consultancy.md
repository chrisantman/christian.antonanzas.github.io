Consulting work in **electroacoustics and room acoustics** carried out as an acoustic engineer (TECESA and Capgemini Engineering), designing sound reinforcement systems and acoustic conditioning for emblematic venues.

International projects include:

- **Ankara Opera Theater** (Turkey) — variable acoustic system (1.1 M€)
- **Saud Bahwan CC Auditorium** (Oman) — PA, cinema and variable acoustic systems (876 k€)
- **Presidential Symphony Orchestra** (Ankara, Turkey) — acoustic analysis of the new building
- **Neşet Ertaş Cultural Center**, **Cemil Meriç Auditorium** and **Hacettepe Olympic Swimming Pool** (Turkey)
- **Mayan Design**, **Jet Kids Jungle Theater** and **Messila home cinema** (Qatar)

National projects include the **Hospital Universitario y Politécnico La Fe** (Valencia), **Hospital Universitario Central de Asturias**, the **Palacio de Congresos de Salamanca**, and venues at the universities of Almería and Granada.

Work involved 3D modelling (SketchUp, Revit), electroacoustic simulation (EASE 4.4), technical proposals, and result reports to optimize coverage, intelligibility and sound quality.
