As **Audio Technical Lead** at [Voicemod](https://www.voicemod.net/) I led the technical audio work in the development of real-time voice processing products, leading the research and application of **digital signal processing (DSP)** algorithms for new audio features.

- Designed and programmed prototypes in MATLAB and Python, then migrated and integrated them into a real-time audio framework in C/C++, including **VST plugin development**.
- Led technical coordination of audio R&D projects, supervised other developers, and planned work with agile methodologies, with a focus on audio quality and real-time performance.

This applied experience in DSP algorithms and audio software architectures is directly transferred to my teaching in acoustics, sound reinforcement, audio processing and audio programming, and to the supervision of final degree and master's theses.
